**Flood-to-Flow AI: Offline Disaster Intelligence for Snapdragon-powered HP AI PCs**

---

## 1. Safety Principles for Disaster Intelligence

Disaster response involves life-and-death triage decisions. AI systems deployed in high-stress operational theaters must never overstep their boundaries or present machine inferences as infallible ground truth.

### Guiding Directives:

1. **AI as Decision Support, Never Decision Replacement**:
   Flood-to-Flow AI functions as a **force multiplier** that extracts, structure, and aggregates evidence to assist human commanders. The system explicitly disclaims automated operational authority.

2. **Mandatory Field Advisory Banner**:
   Every incident summary, on-screen card, and exported PDF brief displays the mandatory advisory:
   > **"AI-assisted assessment — verify critical decisions with trained emergency personnel."**

3. **Transparent, Auditable Point-Scoring**:
   Black-box neural network outputs are dangerous in life-critical settings because rescue workers cannot audit why one sector was prioritized over another. Flood-to-Flow AI employs a deterministic, transparent point decomposition (+30 vulnerable, +25 medical, +20 building, +15 road, +10 water), allowing human incident commanders to verify every single scored point against ground evidence.

4. **No Dangerous Instructions**:
   The tactical recommendation engine adheres to vetted National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) protocols (e.g., standard evacuation asset staging, medical post alerts, power grid de-energization). It never suggests unverified citizen rescue operations or hazardous personal interventions.
